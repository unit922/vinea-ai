## 🔍 PART 2: Let's Find Your Bottlenecks (Friction Level Diagnostics)
*Look at each item below and give it a rating from **1 to 5**. Be honest! Any rating of 3 or higher is a bottleneck where you are losing real money and causing employee stress.*

### 📋 The Rating Scale:
* **Rate 1 (No Hassle / Fully Automated):** Computers and smart tools handle this for you. Your staff does not have to worry about it, and it never fails.
* **Rate 3 (Moderate Drag):** You have a way to do it, but it requires manual typing, takes too much time, or sometimes results in mistakes.
* **Rate 5 (Major Headache / Revenue Leak):** You do this completely by hand. It causes constant stress, takes hours of shift time, or directly causes you to lose sales or waste precious inventory.

---

### 🍇 SECTION A: Wine Cellar & Stock Management
*Keep track of your precious liquid assets without wasting time or losing money.*

#### 1. Slow Bottle Counts (Counting Stock by Hand)
* **What it means:** Your managers or staff have to walk through the cellar with a clipboard, counting wine bottles one by one, or typing numbers laboriously into old spreadsheets.
* **Why it hurts you:** It is incredibly slow, people make typos, and you do not know your real stock count until the end of the month.
* **Rate This Bottleneck [1–5]:** ______

#### 2. Ordering Drinks Based on Guesswork
* **What it means:** When it is time to buy more wine or drinks, you simply guess what to order based on "feeling" rather than counting up upcoming table reservations, local weather trends, or historical fast-sellers.
* **Why it hurts you:** You tie up thousands of dollars of cash flow in slow-moving bottles that just sit on shelves gathering dust, or you run completely out of hot-selling wines on busy resort weekends, leaving guests disappointed.
* **Rate This Bottleneck [1–5]:** ______

#### 3. Pouring Waste & Untracked Depletion (Spillage)
* **What it means:** Bottles get opened, spilled, given as free tasting pours, or over-poured without any easy way to record them. There is no direct link between the physical bottles in the cellar and the cash register.
* **Why it hurts you:** This directly drains your premium margins. Standard restaurants lose about 15% to 20% of their beverage inventory every year to untracked pours.
* **Rate This Bottleneck [1–5]:** ______

### 👑 SECTION B: Guest Care, Service details & Wine Suggestions
*Deliver amazing hospitality that makes guests fall in love with your venue.*

#### 4. Missing Guest Preferences (Knowing VIPs' Taste)
* **What it means:** A high-spending guest or VIP returns to your venue, but your floor staff has no quick, on-screen way to know their favorite grape varietals, past bottle purchase values, allergies, or table seating preferences.
* **Why it hurts you:** Service feels completely generic. You miss the perfect opportunity to offer a custom-tailored VIP experience that drives high-ticket sales and makes them a guest for life.
* **Rate This Bottleneck [1–5]:** ______

#### 5. Forgotten / Weak Follow-ups (Static Messaging)
* **What it means:** You either send zero follow-up emails, or you send rare, generic newsletter announcements to everyone on your marketing list, regardless of what they like to drink or eat.
* **Why it hurts you:** Your promotional emails go unread or straight to the spam box. You cannot easily prompt loyal guests to return for special limited editions or targeted dinner events.
* **Rate This Bottleneck [1–5]:** ______

#### 6. Unprepared Floor Staff & Recommendation Delay
* **What it means:** Your floor servers feel nervous, stressed, or freeze up when guests ask tough questions about a vintage wine, request a customized pairing, or ask for premium recommendations.
* **Why it hurts you:** It slows down service pace, leads to menu order errors, and prevents your team from confidently selling premium, high-margin bottles that guests would genuinely enjoy.
* **Rate This Bottleneck [1–5]:** ______

### ⏱️ SECTION C: Waitstaff Support, Team Schedules & Capacity Management
*Keep your kitchen and dining room teams perfectly coordinated.*

#### 7. Chaos Between Dining Floor & Kitchen Pace
* **What it means:** The dining room gets crowded all at once, the kitchen team gets slammed, and guests wait too long for their appetizers or wines because table seating speeds are unpredictable.
* **Why it hurts you:** Service gets chaotic, your floor staff experiences fast burnout, and your table turn-rate drops—costing you thousands in missed seating capacity each weekend.
* **Rate This Bottleneck [1–5]:** ______

#### 8. Inflexible Staff Schedules / Labor Waste
* **What it means:** It is highly difficult or takes hours to adjust server shift maps and rota schedules when sudden weather changes, cancellations, or peak event bookings occur.
* **Why it hurts you:** You either end up paying extra wages for idle staff during slow shifts, or you are severely understaffed during a surprise rush, leading to poor customer reviews and lost sales.
* **Rate This Bottleneck [1–5]:** ______

#### 9. Long Employee Training & Onboarding Time
* **What it means:** It takes weeks of reading manuals, flashcards, and testing before a newly hired waiter is knowledgeable enough about your wine list and service steps to sell independently on the floor.
* **Why it hurts you:** The high employee turnover rate in hospitality makes long training periods highly expensive. It pulls your busy managers away from active operations and prevents new hires from generating good sales early.
* **Rate This Bottleneck [1–5]:** ______
