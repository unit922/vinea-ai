## 1. The Strategy: Why Vinetelligence fits "The Journey"
The Journey's model is an interactive, high-end 5-course chef's table surprise dining experience. This intimate setting (limited seating, high touch, open-kitchen interaction) makes standard F&B software look cold and mechanical. Vinetelligence solves three hyper-specific challenges for The Journey:

*   **Dynamic Surprise Pairings:** Since the 5-course menu changes based on seasonal and local availability, inventory of premium wine pairings must stay hyper-agile. Checking inventory from clipboards breaks the flow of guest storytelling.
*   **Returning Diner DNA:** At a 18-20 seat chef's table, recognizing a repeat customer and knowing their precise wine preference (e.g., *a preference for complex, earthier Old World structure over bold New World fruit*, or *low-sulfite selections*) creates the ultimate sense of luxury and belonging.
*   **Erase Pairings Friction:** Sommeliers and servers can look up guest palates instantly on a sleek tablet-based Bar Station before the first course is even plated.
